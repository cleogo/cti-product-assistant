# How to Read the CTI Price Masterlist

This document explains the conventions used in the CTI price masterlist so that
quoted prices are interpreted correctly.

## Currency and price basis

All prices are in **Philippine Pesos (PHP, ₱)**. Every price in the masterlist
is a **retail price per unit**, unless the item name states otherwise — for
example `COTTON GLOVES 8# (price per pair)` is priced per pair, and
`Foley Catheter for Adults - 10pcs per box` is priced per box of ten.

Prices are stored as whole pesos. There are no centavo values in the
masterlist; a price shown as ₱485 means ₱485.00.

## What the price does and does not include

The masterlist records the **retail list price only**. It does not encode:

- Volume or dealer discounts
- Delivery, freight, or installation charges
- Customization surcharges (several sports items are made to order)
- Taxes, if quoted separately by the sales team

Anyone asking for a landed or delivered cost should be told the list price and
reminded that delivery and discounts are quoted separately by the sales team.

## Bundled items

Some entries include a free bundled accessory, stated in the item name. Fire
extinguishers in the `FIR-002` group are the most common case — wall-type
extinguishers ship with a free wall bracket or wall hook. The bundled item is
part of the quoted price, not an add-on.

## Certification markings

Items marked **WITH OSHC** are compliant with Occupational Safety and Health
Center requirements. This matters for Philippine workplace-compliance buyers:
where a site requires OSHC-compliant PPE, only the OSHC-marked variants
qualify. The masterlist usually carries both an ordinary and an OSHC variant of
the same item at different prices — for example ordinary hard hats versus
`HD HELMET (WITH OSHC)`.

## Colors

Where an item is offered in multiple colors, the colors are listed on the
record and the **price is the same across all colors**. A color choice does not
change the price. If only one color is listed, that is the only color stocked.

## Sizes and variants

Size and capacity variants are almost always **separate products with separate
codes**, not options on one product. A 23-litre push trash bin and a 58-litre
push trash bin are two different codes at two different prices. When someone
asks about "the trash bin," the correct response is to ask which capacity, or
to list the available capacities with their prices.

## Price validity

The masterlist is a point-in-time export. Prices change. Any quotation given to
a customer should be confirmed against the current masterlist before it is
committed to in writing.
