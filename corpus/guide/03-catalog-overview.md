# What CTI Sells — Catalog Overview

CTI supplies **workplace safety, facility, and institutional equipment**. The
masterlist holds 1,038 priced items across twelve departments. The typical
buyer is an organisation outfitting a site: a construction project, a factory,
a school, a barangay or LGU, a clinic, or an office fit-out.

Prices range from ₱10 (a hair net) to ₱885,830 (a customised wrestling mat).
The median item is ₱2,710.

## Departments

### Furniture — 303 items, the largest department
Office fit-out: tables and desks (₱3,520–₱50,950), chairs and seating
(₱2,530–₱25,210), and pedestals, cabinets and storage (₱5,320–₱28,340).
Tables and chairs are listed with explicit dimensions in centimetres and, for
chairs, upholstery colour and frame type. This is the department to look at for
a whole-office or whole-classroom outfit.

### Janitorial — 213 items
Cleaning equipment (₱190–₱42,900) including mop squeezers in many capacities,
plus trash bins and waste containers (₱870–₱29,760). Both groups are heavily
size-segmented: bins run from small push bins up to 240-litre outdoor units.

### PPE — 185 items
Personal protective equipment worn on the body: safety and reflective vests,
gloves, face and welding protection, goggles, hearing protection, rainwear,
helmets, hard hats, and whistles. This is the highest-volume, lowest-unit-cost
department — many items are under ₱500 — and the department where OSHC
certification matters most.

### Fire Safety — 108 items
Fire extinguishers (₱570–₱44,280) in dry chemical, AFFF, HCFC and CO₂ types,
from 1 lb wall units up to 50 lb wheeled units, plus general fire equipment
(₱200–₱63,280) such as fire blankets, hoses, and oxygen tanks. Extinguisher
codes encode both weight and chemical type.

### Traffic & Site Safety — 64 items
Traffic cones (₱340–₱18,520) in PVC and rubber at several heights, and
barriers, plastic and steel chains, and caution tape (₱340–₱3,630). Used for
road works, parking control, and site demarcation.

### Footwear — 37 items
Safety shoes (₱1,180–₱2,180), low cut and high cut variants, and work boots
(₱340–₱1,210) including rain boots and steel-toe boots. Cut style (high/low)
and OSHC certification are the two attributes buyers usually specify.

### Sports — 37 items
Martial arts and gym equipment: sanda uniforms and protective gear, resistance
bands, mats and weightlifting platforms. Contains the catalog's most expensive
items, several of which are customised and made to order.

### Rescue — 31 items
Water rescue and life saving (₱300–₱16,270) — life rings, life jackets in
adult and kids sizes, rescue tubes — plus first aid kits (₱650–₱5,800) and
modular tents.

### Fall Protection — 26 items
Full-body harnesses (₱340–₱2,060) and lanyards (₱640–₱6,410), including
shock-absorber variants. Almost always bought together, and the OSHC-marked
variants are the ones that satisfy site compliance checks.

### Medical — 18 items
Clinic and training equipment: training manikins, surgical instrument sets,
stethoscopes, hospital linens, catheters and bedpans. Spans ₱60 to ₱43,470.

### Equipment — 13 items
Standalone machines and devices: flashlights and headlights, megaphones,
portable toilets, and vending and coffee machines (₱361,290–₱504,900, the
highest-priced non-sports items in the catalog).

### Security — 3 items
Barangay tanod vest, cap and baton.

## Cross-department buying patterns

Several common purchases span departments, and a good answer should mention the
related items rather than only the one asked about:

- **Construction site outfit** — hard hats (HAR), safety vests (SAF-001),
  safety shoes (SAF-002), gloves (PPE-005), harnesses and lanyards (BOD-001,
  LAN-001), traffic cones and caution tape (PPE-012, PPE-014).
- **Working-at-height compliance** — a harness alone is incomplete; it pairs
  with a lanyard, and both should be the OSHC-marked variants.
- **Welding station** — welding mask (FAC-001), welding gloves (PPE-005),
  welding apron (BOD-002).
- **Office fit-out** — tables (FUR-001), chairs (FUR-002), pedestals (FUR-003),
  plus trash bins (TRA-001) and janitorial supplies (JAN-001).
- **Clinic or school infirmary** — medical equipment (MED-001) plus first aid
  kits (LIF-002).
- **Barangay or LGU emergency response** — first aid kits, life jackets, life
  rings, rescue tubes, megaphones, modular tents, tanod equipment.
